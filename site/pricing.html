FAQ

Pricing questionsPertanyaan soal harga

How do I get the 20% early-bird discount?Bagaimana cara dapat diskon early-bird 20%?

Join the waitlist before launch. When we open checkout, the discount is applied automatically to the email you registered — for the first 12 months of any paid plan.Gabung waitlist sebelum rilis. Saat checkout dibuka, diskon otomatis berlaku untuk email yang kamu daftarkan — selama 12 bulan pertama di paket berbayar mana pun.

What happens if I run out of tokens?Bagaimana kalau token habis?

Agents pause — nothing breaks. Top up, upgrade, or wait for your monthly refill.Agent berhenti sementara — tidak ada yang rusak. Top-up, upgrade, atau tunggu isi ulang bulanan.

Can I cancel anytime?Bisa berhenti kapan saja?

Yes. Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period. See our refund policy in the Terms.Bisa. Paketmu tetap aktif sampai akhir periode tagihan. Lihat kebijakan refund di Syarat & Ketentuan.

Do tokens include AI model costs?Apakah token sudah termasuk biaya model AI?

Yes. Tokens cover model usage, orchestration and storage. With BYOK (Pro and Studio), model calls are billed by your provider and only orchestration uses tokens.Ya. Token mencakup pemakaian model, orkestrasi, dan penyimpanan. Dengan BYOK (Pro dan Studio), panggilan model ditagih oleh penyedia kamu dan hanya orkestrasi yang memakai token.

Are Roblox upload fees included?Apakah biaya upload Roblox sudah termasuk?

No. Any Robux fees Roblox charges (for example to upload clothing or publish UGC) are paid by you on Roblox. Our moderation pre-check helps you avoid paying for rejected uploads.Tidak. Biaya Robux yang dikenakan Roblox (misalnya upload baju atau publish UGC) kamu bayar di Roblox. Pre-check moderasi kami membantu menghindari bayar untuk upload yang ditolak.
